# The format study of 2026-11-16 and its three reviews

The records [ADR 0016](../../adr/0016-format-8-is-frozen.md) decided on,
copied here as they were written, by other sessions than the one that
wrote the ADR. Paths in them under `/tmp` and `~/work` name those
sessions' scratch and ada, and are not kept.

- [writeup.txt](writeup.txt): the study, six candidate additions to walk
  format 8 and the baseline of changing nothing, each with its need, its
  smallest form inside the file and as a side file, and its measured cost
- [method-preregistered.txt](method-preregistered.txt): the evidence
  standard the study fixed before it measured
- [records.jsonl](records.jsonl): the measurements the write-up cites as
  `R<n>`, a line each; [candidates.jsonl](candidates.jsonl): the
  write-up's claims a candidate each, with the records behind them
- [review-need.txt](review-need.txt), [review-routes.txt](review-routes.txt)
  and [review-spec.txt](review-spec.txt): the three reviews, on whether
  each candidate is needed before the freeze, by which route a reader
  would get it, and whether its text and checks are complete; each
  opens with the criteria it wrote first, which
  [criteria-need.txt](criteria-need.txt),
  [criteria-routes.txt](criteria-routes.txt) and
  [criteria-spec.txt](criteria-spec.txt) hold alone
- [pggbY.paths.tsv](pggbY.paths.tsv): the names of pggb chrY's path
  lines, from the review of graph-walks' counting call, which the open
  problem on the haplotypes a file lists counts
